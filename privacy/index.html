Privacy Policy

Koz — Batak Card Game · Last updated 9 July 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information the Koz mobile game (“the App”, “we”), published by Zübeyir Topuş, handles and how. Koz is designed to need as little personal data as possible: you can play without an account, and we do not ask for your name, email, or any real-world identity.

Information we do NOT collect

  • No sign-up, no account, no email or password.
  • No real name, phone number, address, or contacts.
  • The nickname and avatar you pick are stored on your device and are used only to label your seat during online matches.

Information handled while you play

  • Anonymous session (online play): for multiplayer we create an anonymous session with a random identifier via Supabase. It contains no personal information.
  • Multiplayer gameplay: your chosen nickname, avatar and in-game moves are sent to our game server so matches can run in real time. They are not used to identify you personally. Match results are stored only with random identifiers and display nicknames — no personal identity.
  • Advertising: free players see ads via Google AdMob. AdMob may use device identifiers to show and measure ads. On iOS you are asked for permission (App Tracking Transparency) before any tracking — you can decline and still play; ads then remain non‑personalised.
  • Ad consent in the EEA, UK and Switzerland: where GDPR and similar laws apply, the App shows Google’s consent form before any ad is loaded. You choose whether your data may be used for personalised advertising, and you can change or withdraw your choice at any time via Settings → Ad privacy options in the App. If you do not consent, the App still works normally — ads may be limited or not shown at all.
  • Purchases: subscriptions are processed by Apple and managed through RevenueCat. We never see your payment details. RevenueCat receives an anonymous app user id and your subscription status so the App can unlock premium.
  • Analytics & diagnostics: we use PostHog (EU region) to collect anonymous usage events (e.g. which screens are opened) and basic error reports to improve the game. These carry an app-generated anonymous identifier and are not tied to your identity; you can turn this off in the App’s Settings.

Children

Koz is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from them.

Third‑party services

The App uses Apple, Google AdMob, RevenueCat, Supabase and PostHog. Their handling of data is governed by their own privacy policies, linked above.

Your choices

  • Decline ad tracking in the iOS permission prompt (Settings → Privacy → Tracking).
  • In the EEA, UK and Switzerland: change or withdraw your ad-consent choice anytime via Settings → Ad privacy options in the App.
  • Turn off anonymous analytics in the App’s Settings.
  • Subscribe to Premium to remove ads entirely.
  • Since we hold no account data, uninstalling the App removes everything stored on your device. For questions about anonymous server-side match records, contact us at the address below.

Changes

We may update this policy; the “Last updated” date above will change accordingly.
